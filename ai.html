BLEVE Peak Pressure Prediction

A machine-learning regression project that predicts peak pressure in Boiling Liquid Expanding Vapour Explosion (BLEVE) scenarios from experimental and sensor data.

Role: Machine-learning project contributor

Approach: Built a regression workflow covering data cleaning, feature engineering, model comparison, hyperparameter tuning, and evaluation.

  • Compared Random Forest, Support Vector Regression, XGBoost, and neural network models
  • Handled missing values, duplicates, categorical features, and outliers
  • Evaluated predictions with MAPE and R² and explored an ensemble approach

Alibaba Cloud AI Hackathon - AI AdForge

Collaborated on a generative-AI marketing workflow that creates short, branded promotional videos. The concept was developed around an Al Fardan Exchange campaign and supports Arabic and English content.

Team: Saha Rathor, Zernish Nazir, and Natale De Silva

Workflow: Alibaba Cloud Qwen generates campaign copy and scene prompts; Wan creates short video clips that are assembled with captions, subtitles, and branding.

  • Wan 2.2 text-to-image and image-to-video models for individual scenes
  • Scene-by-scene generation improves coherence; text overlays are added during video editing
